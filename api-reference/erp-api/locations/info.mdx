---
title: "Locations"
---
A location is a place the ERP tracks inventory in or transacts from — a warehouse, store, production line, or consignment site. Locations may be nested via `parent_location_id`; `full_path` shows the hierarchy. Location ids appear throughout the ERP API: on sales- and purchase-order lines, on shipments and receipts, and as the keys of `inventory.inventory_by_location_id`.
