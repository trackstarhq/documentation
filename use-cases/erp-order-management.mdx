---
title: "ERP Order Management"
description: "Sync sales and purchase orders, inventory, and master data from ERP systems"
---

The ERP API gives you a single, normalized interface over ERP platforms (such as
NetSuite) for order management, inventory, and procurement. Read sales orders,
purchase orders, and their fulfillment events; keep master data (items,
locations, customers, vendors, employees) in sync; and write orders back.
